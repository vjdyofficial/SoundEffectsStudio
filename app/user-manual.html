Can't play audio
If the sound is not playing, it may be due to the following reasons:
  • The audio file is not supported by the app.
  • The audio file is corrupted or missing.
  • Your device's volume is muted or too low.
  • There is a technical issue with the app.

Technically the AudioContext don't have issues anymore on audio routing which is already fixed before 3.x versions.
How effects actually works?

Surround Sound

Surround Sound effect simulates a surround sound experience by manipulating the audio channels. It creates a more immersive listening experience by making it feel like the sound is coming from different directions.

Surround Center

A effect that simulates the mono channel that is included with surround sound setups. It improves vocal hearing if you don't hear the mid and high frequency properly.

Surround Threshold

A effect that increases the area of environment or micro-threshold which surround sound heard in specific area. Can increase the gain and cause loudness! Please use it wisely! or consider changing up the master volume.

Bass

A effect that simulates the subwoofer effect that is included with surround sound setups. It improves Bass Quality, You can actually create presets in it!

Fader

If you're famillar with Sound System setups in your area, this effect give you a Gain Control in Mid and High Frequency, which useful to isolate that frequency on host voiceovers! For this effect to work, you need a Bass Effect enabled by setting up Bass Gain to more than 0.1.

Reverb

A effect that sounds immersive on some different room areas far away from your speaker to your ears in actually.

Equalizer

Improves the frequencies of the sounds by adjusting the levels of different frequency bands. 8 bands included from 125Hz to 16kHz.

Import Methods
There are two methods to import media file into the app:
Storage Access Framework
Click on this icon on selected Audio Deck or Media Deck.
Scroll all the way and choose to import media.
Click on Open.

Now you are ready to play your imported media.

Be sure the Operation Controls UI, along with Performance Media Decks page is active and did this procedure.
Drag and Drop
This method is recommended for Desktop devices.

Simply open your File Explorer, navigate to the directory where your audio files are stored, select the file you want to import.


Drag it into the Audio Decks A, B, C, or D. or Media Decks A or B.

Now you are ready to play your imported media.


You can now also Import the media file by drag and dropping it to the Sound Buttons (center), and it shows the dialog to see which deck you have to import. The app will highlight the deck and navigate which deck you are import with.
