Can't get the audio list into sampler
If the sound buttons are not showing, means that the sound effects are not loaded yet. Here are the following tips:
Automatic Install

To download automatically without visiting GitHub to download the SFX Pack,

Go to More Options
Scroll all the way to Cumulative Sound Effect Pack Info.
Click on Install Pack or Update Pack.

This will exit the app to run Windows Powershell for installation.


It's recommended to NOT open the app during installation!


If installation fails during session, You must proceed to Manual Install.
Manual Install
Download the Cumulative Sound Effects Pack from GitHub, download the asset called sfx.zip which is the latest, and extract the sfx folder in the correct directory which is

[your path] /SFXStudio-win32-x64/resources/app/

the audio files are located in the sfx folder of the app.

[your path] /SFXStudio-win32-x64/resources/app/sfx

Be sure that the list.js is in the folder, otherwise the app will not get the audio to fetch into the sampler.
Can't play audio
If the sound is not playing, it may be due to the following reasons:
  • The audio file is not supported by the app.
  • The audio file is corrupted or missing.
  • Your device's volume is muted or too low.
  • There is a technical issue with the app.

Since list.js is the main script that fetches the audio files, you can modify to see the directory of the audio files. Feel free to edit the list.js file for directory changes or to add more audio files.

Well if the samples are fetched but you can't still hear the audio while playing, means that there is a issue with AudioContext due to browser security policy. or worse, due to lack of performance of your device that cause AudioContext to stop working properly. If occurs, please restart the app.

How effects actually works?

Surround Sound

Surround Sound effect simulates a surround sound experience by manipulating the audio channels. It creates a more immersive listening experience by making it feel like the sound is coming from different directions.

This feature is based on what can do in Poweramp Stereo Expand. It actually uses Gain to Merge the Normal Gain and the Negative Gain.


Gain -1 = Inverse Polarity Gain +1 = Normal Polarity

This means the the Inverse Polarity will invert all mids and high frequencies to simulate a surround sound effect.

  • For Stereo Audio (2 channels), it will create a pseudo surround sound effect by adjusting the volume and panning of the left and right channels.
  • For sound systems with Dolby Powered SRS will still work but improves surround more.
  • This feature will not work on Mono Audio (1 channel) as the Stereo Channel is required.

That's how Surround Soudn works on the app. Pretty basic but so literal.

Equalizer

Improves the frequencies of the sounds by adjusting the levels of different frequency bands.

The 10-band equalizer lets you adjust the gain (volume) of specific frequency ranges in the audio signal. Each slider represents a frequency band, from low bass (31Hz) to high treble (16kHz).

  • Low Pass (31Hz, 62Hz, 125Hz): Controls the bass frequencies. Increasing these makes the sound deeper and more powerful.
  • Mid Pass (250Hz, 500Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz): Controls the midrange, affecting vocals and most instruments.
  • High Pass (4kHz, 8kHz, 16kHz): Controls the treble, making the sound brighter or duller.

Move each slider to boost (+) or cut (–) the volume of its frequency band. This helps you shape the overall sound to your preference or to suit different environments.

The equalizer works in real-time, so changes are immediately reflected in the audio output.

Import Methods
There are two methods to import media file into the app:
Storage Access Framework
Click on this icon on selected Audio Deck or Media Deck.
Scroll all the way and choose to import media.
Click on Open.

Now you are ready to play your imported media.

Be sure the Operation Controls UI, along with Performance Media Decks page is active and did this procedure.
Drag and Drop
This method is recommended for Desktop devices.

Simply open your File Explorer, navigate to the directory where your audio files are stored, select the file you want to import.


Be sure the Operation Controls UI, along with Performance Media Decks page is active, and drag it into the Audio Decks A, B, C, or D. or Media Decks A or B.

Now you are ready to play your imported media.
